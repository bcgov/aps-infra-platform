
My Self Serve is new to SDX, so Riley receives an email welcoming him to SDX and receives a followup onboarding meeting with SDX Operations.

Harley includes Michael, an Application Manager in the Ministry of SDPR, to the meeting.

If organization onboarding is required, Michael will go through the same process that Riley went through for the Ministry of Health to onboard onto SDX.

Michael will be assigned the system administrator role.  He will setup My Self Serve as a subsystem, specifying a short unique identifier within the organization, and a meaningful description.

Like Riley, he also decides to use the shared Edge Runtime.

To complete subsystem setup, Michael must link the Common SSO Integration ID to the My Self Serve record in SDX.

Michael completes team access by adding Harley as the Tech Lead.
