Harley is new to SDX, so he receives an email welcoming him to SDX and receives a followup onboarding meeting with SDX Operations.

Harley includes Lucy, the SDX System Administrator for Ministry of SDPR, to the meeting.

If organization onboarding is required, Lucy will go through the same process that was done for Ministry of Health to onboard onto SDX.

Lucy can now setup her first subsystem, specifying a short unique identifier within the organization, and a meaningful description.

She registers the subsystem on the Shared Edge Runtime.

For Identity Link, Lucy must link the Subsystem that the Integration belongs to.

She assigns teams access to the subsystem.  This team will perform the day-to-day activities for the service.  Harley as the Tech Lead.  She may also want to specify the subsystem owner (product owner).
