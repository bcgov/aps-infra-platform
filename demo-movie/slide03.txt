The scenario in this demo centers around the BC Employment and Assistance program.

Specifically a person applying for assistance using the My Self Serve application.
For verifying eligibility during the application process, the Patient Health Number (PHN) is required.

The Provincial Client Registry is the authoritative source for the PHN, so instead of requiring the user to search around for their PHN and
enter it into the form, the My Self Serve application will use SDX to call the Client Registry and include the PHN in the application for
assistance.

The Provincial Client Registry is operated by the Ministry of Health, and My Self Serve is operated by the Ministry of Social Development and
Poverty Reduction.
