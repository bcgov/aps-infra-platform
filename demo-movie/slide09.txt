With Lucy assigned the administration role on SDX, she is now able to manage the subsystem that
will be representing the services offered by the Provincial Client Registry by the Ministry of Health.

Setting up a subsystem requires two key decisions:

1. the Edge Runtime that it will be operating on
2. the Identity privacy zone that it will operated in

Lucy makes a decision to use the Shared Edge Runtime provided by APS, so SDX Operations applies the permission to allow her organization to use the Edge Runtime “share0”.

The Provincial Client Registry is an existing Health system, and currently operates in the Citizen Health privacy zone, so the subystem will be updated
with the correct PZ.  The selected privacy zone is reviewed by the Identity Assurance team to ensure that the subsystem is correctly placed.

The PCR subsystem has a team to support it - so Lucy will assign individuals to the roles: Subsystem Owner, Tech Lead and Access Manager.

Lucy now has all the information required to setup the new subsystem for Provincial Client Registry.
