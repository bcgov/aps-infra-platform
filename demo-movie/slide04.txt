To help with this demo, we will use five personas.

Riley, a Data Architect in the Ministry of Health responsible for ensuring that the technical structure, models, and system behavior underpinning a program dataset are sound, consistent, and aligned with enterprise architecture standards. 

Janis, a full-stack developer working in the Ministry of Health on the Provincial Client Registry Team.

Mark, a Security Manager working in Operations, responsible for approving access to the Provincial Client Registry.

Michael, an Application Manager for the Ministry of SDPR, is responsible for the ongoing operation, support, and maintenance of systems that consume data from other ministries and programs.
