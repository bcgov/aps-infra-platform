What is a scope? Scope is a mechanism that lets a client application request a limited set of permissions to access a user's account or protected resources.

How do scopes work?

Permission Boundaries: Scopes act as labels or constraints attached to an access token. They tell the resource server (the API) exactly what actions the application is allowed to perform

User Consent: When an app requests authorization, the requested scopes appear on the user's consent screen. Users see what data or actions the app wants (e.g., reading emails versus posting updates) and can approve or deny them.

What is a “Privacy Zone”?

Information across distinct government databases (such as medical history, driving records, and tax files) is not linked or cross-accessible by different authorities through your login.

This is accomplished by grouping related systems into privacy zones where the user’s identifier is different in each privacy zone.
